How TalentSphere works

One workflow for both sides of hiring: candidates discover and apply, recruiters post and review.

For job seekers

  1. 1

    Create Your Profile

    Add your skills, qualification and experience so we can suggest roles that match.

  2. 2

    Discover Opportunities

    Search, filter and save jobs. Open any listing to read the full details.

  3. 3

    Apply for Jobs

    Send a validated application with a short cover letter in a couple of minutes.

  4. 4

    Track Your Progress

    Watch each application move from submitted to shortlisted, interview and selected.

For recruiters

  1. 1

    Create a recruiter demo profile

    Start a demo recruiter session. No real account is created.

  2. 2

    Post a job

    Fill in the role details and publish. It appears in Find Jobs straight away.

  3. 3

    Review applications

    Search candidates, filter by status and open each application.

  4. 4

    Update candidate status

    Move people through the pipeline. Job seekers see the change in their dashboard.

About TalentSphere

TalentSphere is a recruitment platform concept built for a college software engineering project. Its mission is to make hiring clearer for everyone involved: candidates know where they stand, and recruiters can act on applications without friction.

Accessibility
Keyboard-friendly, readable and usable on any screen size.
Efficiency
Search, apply and review in a few clicks, with no clutter in the way.
Transparency
Every application shows its current status and history.
Usability
Plain language, clear feedback and forms that explain their errors.

For job seekers

  • Search and filter listings by role, place, type, level and salary.
  • Save jobs, apply with one form and keep a profile ready.
  • See clear status updates for every application.

For recruiters

  • Post, edit and remove jobs from one workspace.
  • Review candidates, search them and filter by status.
  • Spot bottlenecks with simple built-in analytics.

What this prototype is, and is not

TalentSphere is a frontend-only prototype. Jobs, applications and profile edits are stored in your browser's localStorage, not in a database. Demo logins are not secure authentication, resumes are never uploaded, and no emails or interview invitations are sent. A real deployment would need a secure backend, a database, server-side validation, authentication, authorization and safe file handling.
